# Advanced Capes 9minecraft Mod - Wear Any Image As a Cape

Advanced Capes 9minecraft Mod lets you wear any image from the internet as a custom cape. You do not need to upload files or link an account to change your look. You just paste a direct web link to a picture, and it appears on your back in the game. This works in both single-player worlds and multiplayer servers. Other players who have this installed will see your chosen design, and you will see theirs. Features: Custom URL Capes: Paste a direct link to any PNG image online to wear it instantly.
